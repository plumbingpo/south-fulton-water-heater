Kitchen Hot Water

Restaurant and Food Service Water Heaters

Restaurants and food-service businesses have hot water demands that ordinary equipment can't meet. Dish machines, three-compartment sinks, prep sinks, mop sinks, and handwashing stations all draw at once during a busy dinner service, and an undersized heater falls behind just as the dish room fills up. South Fulton Water Heater handles food-service hot water, including high-input gas tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters for high-temperature dish machines, correct sizing by peak-hour demand, and scheduled service that works around your hours.

If the main heater falls behind, handwashing sinks go lukewarm, the three-compartment sink falls below temperature, and the dish machine can't hit its final rinse at peak service. Our technicians verify temperatures from the tank all the way to the fixtures and log every visit in written reports your inspector and landlord can review.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing after close when needed, keeping downtime short, and keeping part of the system online where possible.

Safety

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Badly installed or neglected hot water equipment can create carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, bacterial growth in tanks stored too cool, and costly water damage from a ruptured tank. Those are real risks for a commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Our commercial installations and inspections cover venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, followed by a written report that ranks each risk by priority and recommends specific fixes.

Regular professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and acting on expert recommendations keeps small issues from becoming emergencies. It also creates a record. We maintain service logs, temperature records, and a documentation binder for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and inspectors that you've done your due diligence.

Emergency Service

After-Hours Commercial Water Heater Response

When a commercial water heater fails, the clock starts immediately. Kitchens can't sanitize, salons turn clients away, hotel guests complain, and tenants start calling. Calls to our commercial line get a technician rolling quickly for emergency repairs throughout South Fulton and the surrounding area, and planned work can be booked after hours to limit downtime.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. The cause could be a failed gas valve, a burned-out element, a flame sensor fault, a tripped high-limit switch, a leaking tank, a stalled recirculation pump, or a tankless bank stuck in lockout. Whatever it is, we diagnose it fast, get hot water flowing again, and record every step for your facility files. Afterward, we put maintenance in place to keep it from happening again.

Maintenance Programs

Proactive Water Heater Care for Businesses

The most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters is preventing failures before they happen. South Fulton Water Heater offers customized maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes. Our programs include scheduled inspections, tank flushing, anode rod checks, tankless descaling, relief valve testing, burner and venting inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve checks, and temperature verification at the fixtures. Each program is tailored to the property's equipment, water hardness, usage patterns, and budget.

Fewer breakdowns, longer-lasting tanks, and lower energy costs: those are the results a maintenance program is built to deliver. A program typically costs a fraction of a single emergency replacement. Call South Fulton Water Heater to build a plan around your property's needs and budget.
