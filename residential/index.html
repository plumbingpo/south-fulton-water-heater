No Surprises

Clear Pricing Before Any Work Starts

Nobody likes surprises on the final bill. So South Fulton Water Heater quotes every job upfront, after a complete diagnostic that covers the heater's age, fuel type, and capacity, the burner assembly or heating elements, the anode rod and relief valve, and the vent and gas connections. You'll know the problem, what the fix involves, and what it costs before you say yes.

Most water heater failures give quiet warnings long before the tank quits. Watch for hot water with a rusty tint, a rumbling tank, a sulfur smell from the hot tap, or showers that go cold sooner than they used to. Behind those symptoms are usually heavy sediment, a spent anode rod, or a failing dip tube. An early service call at the first sign usually costs far less than mopping up after a ruptured tank.

Not every visit is about a breakdown. Many homeowners ask us to size up an aging heater before it fails, and it's a smart move. With a typical tank lifespan of 8 to 12 years (tankless units often run 20 years with annual descaling), a replacement on your own timeline beats a rushed swap after a leak. We'll give you an honest read on how much life your unit has left.

Maintenance Plans

Tank Flushing and Tune-Ups That Pay Off

Preventing a breakdown beats paying for one. That's the idea behind our annual maintenance. Once a year, we drain and flush the tank, measure the anode rod, test the relief valve, inspect the venting and gas connections or the electrical components, and descale tankless heat exchangers, all matched to the heater in your home.

We offer flexible maintenance options for homeowners, with annual service for most tanks and twice-yearly flushing where the water is hard. That's especially important where mineral content is high, where sediment coats the tank floor, makes the burner work harder, and wears out the tank. Each home is assessed individually, and the plan is tailored to your heater's age, your household's hot water demands, and your local water.

Consider maintenance as a safeguard for equipment you've already bought. Each visit keeps the tank clear of sediment, the anode rod doing its job, and the relief valve ready to open, and that lowers monthly energy bills while reducing wear on the burner or elements. For most households, maintenance costs far less than replacing the heater ahead of schedule.

Proper Sizing

A Water Heater Sized for Your Actual Household

An undersized water heater runs out before everyone has showered, while an oversized one burns fuel keeping extra gallons hot around the clock. South Fulton Water Heater sizes every replacement and new installation to your actual household. For tank models, that means matching the first-hour rating to your morning rush, rather than simply copying the gallon size on the old tank. For tankless models, it means sizing the gallons per minute to the fixtures you run at once and the temperature of your incoming water.

Households change over time. A new baby, a teenager, or an in-law suite can overwhelm the tank that used to be plenty. Tankless water heaters heat water on demand and don't run out as long as the flow stays within its GPM rating, while heat pump water heaters use far less electricity than a standard electric tank. If you're torn between tank and tankless, or gas and electric, we'll lay out the honest pros and cons in installed price, operating cost, venting and gas line needs, and installation work.

Frequent Failures

The Water Heater Repairs Local Homes Need Most

Homes in South Fulton run into a familiar set of water heater failures, and each one calls for a different fix. South Fulton Water Heater handles them all: no hot water, water that never gets hot enough, pilots that won't stay lit, electric tanks that trip the breaker, noisy tanks full of sediment, rusty or smelly water, leaking relief valves, and tankless units flashing error codes. Our technicians identify the exact fault and target the repair to the component that actually failed.

Some failures can wait a day, and some can't. A failed element just cuts your hot water supply. A relief valve spitting water, on the other hand, signals that pressure or temperature inside the tank is too high, and a slow leak at the base can soak into flooring before anyone notices. If you smell gas, get everyone outside and call the gas utility first. Our technicians carry the parts to swap elements, thermostats, thermocouples, gas valves, and relief valves, add a thermal expansion tank when pressure is the culprit, and make leak repairs at fittings and drain valves.
